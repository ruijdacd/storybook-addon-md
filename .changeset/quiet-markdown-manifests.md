Add opt-in documentation manifests with original Markdown, frontmatter summaries, and live updates for standalone and attached docs. Enable `manifests: true` to use [Storybook 10.6.0 manifests](https://storybook.js.org/docs/ai/manifests) and [@storybook/addon-mcp](https://storybook.js.org/docs/ai/mcp/overview) without a custom preset.

See the [setup guide](https://github.com/ruijdacd/storybook-addon-md#documentation-manifests-and-mcp) and [examples with and without MCP](https://github.com/ruijdacd/storybook-addon-md#examples-and-contributing).
