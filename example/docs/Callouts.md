## Callouts

GitHub-style alerts render as labelled callouts. Ordinary blockquotes are unchanged.

> [!NOTE]
> Additional context that helps readers understand the surrounding text.

> [!TIP]
> Recommended approach with **emphasis**, `inline code`, and a
> link to the [introduction](./Introduction.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Information readers need to succeed.
>
> - Check every requirement.
> - Follow the steps in order.

> [!WARNING]
> Something that requires care.
>
> ```ts
> const confirmed = window.confirm('Continue?');
> ```

> [!CAUTION]
> A risk or destructive consequence, such as deleting a branch.

> A plain quotation stays a quotation.

> [!FOOTNOTE]
> Unrecognized markers stay as written.
